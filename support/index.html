Naila’s Orbit Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to nailasorbit.help@gmail.com. I usually reply within two working days.

When you report a problem, please add your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number (shown on the map).

How to play

You circle a ring. Tap anywhere on the screen to leap straight ahead to the next ring, and reach the bud to make it bloom. Collect the seeds of light: they open the next garden. On the first two levels a ghost hand shows you the timing. If a level is hard, it runs a little slower after several tries, and then you can skip it (a garden’s final only after more tries, and never the very last level).

Language

Settings (gear icon) → Language. The game plays in English, Azerbaijani, Turkish, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Russian and Brazilian Portuguese.

Progress

Your progress is stored on your iPhone. It moves to a new iPhone when you restore an iCloud or computer backup; there is no account and no cloud sync. Settings → Reset progress erases it (settings, Glowspores and light trails stay; lantern colors light up again with seeds). A level never pays the same Glowspores twice, even after a reset.

Ads

All 200 levels are free. After the first garden, a short ad may come between levels after some wins: at most 3 a session and 6 a day, at least 3 minutes apart, never after you lose, in a Thorn trial or in the daily garden.

This version sells nothing: there are no in-app purchases.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Settings → About → Privacy choices changes your answer to Google's consent form.

Report an ad

An ad that should not be there (not suitable for everyone, misleading, broken)? Tap Google's ad options icon in the ad's corner to report it to Google, and please write to nailasorbit.help@gmail.com too, with the day and time and what the ad showed: I will block it.

Accessibility

  • Music, sound effects and vibration can each be turned off in Settings.
  • The whole game is played with one finger and one kind of tap.
  • Menus and buttons have VoiceOver labels in all nine languages. The gameplay itself needs sight and precise timing, so it cannot be played with VoiceOver alone.
  • Hazards differ by shape and movement as well as color.

Privacy

Naila’s Orbit has no account and collects no data itself. The ads come from Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage data to show them; the game never asks to track you, and under-13s get Google's child treatment. Without a connection the game plays fully (with no ads). Full policy: https://abbasasadov1-coder.github.io/privacy
