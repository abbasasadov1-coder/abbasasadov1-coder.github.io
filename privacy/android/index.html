Naila’s Orbit Privacy Policy (Android)

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Naila’s Orbit is a game made by Abbas Asadov. This policy covers the Android version of Naila’s Orbit on Google Play. The iPhone version has its own policy: https://abbasasadov1-coder.github.io/privacy.

In short: the game has no account and no server of its own, and it does not collect your data itself. It shows short ads from Google AdMob after some wins and after many lost tries, and Google's software collects some device and usage data to show them. If you use Google Play Games, your achievements and Thorn trial times go to it. The Android version sells nothing: there are no in-app purchases.

What the game stores, and where

  • Your progress (cleared levels, seeds, best times, medals, Glowspores and light trails), your settings (language, sound, vibration, lantern color), the ads' own counts (sessions, ads shown, your age group) and, for the optional reminders, your choice and the hours at which you last opened the game are stored only on your phone, in the game's private storage. They are never sent to me.
  • If Android's backup is on, your save file (progress and settings, nothing else) is included in your own Google backup, so a reinstall or a new phone brings your progress back. It goes to your Google account, never to me; you control it in your phone's settings (Google → Backup).
  • Uninstalling the game deletes this data from the phone. Settings → Reset progress erases your progress (your settings, Glowspores and trails and the age answer are kept).

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • A short full-screen ad may come after some wins (once the flower has bloomed, before the result) or after many lost or restarted tries (the level first holds for a second under an "Ad break…" notice). Ads begin only after about five minutes of play and five levels won; Google's ad software starts only at that point. There are never ads in the daily garden or in the middle of play. Optional rewarded ads can unlock Skip or a garden Pass sooner; both also become free after more tries.
  • To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK and its consent SDK (User Messaging Platform) collect: your approximate location from the IP address, how the app and the ads are used (for example app launches, taps and ad views), diagnostics (for example launch time, hangs and energy use) and device identifiers (Android's advertising ID and the app set ID). Google uses them to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse and to improve its SDK; the data is encrypted in transit. The game itself reads none of it. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • On phones with Android's Privacy Sandbox, Google's SDK may also use its ad APIs (Topics and Attribution reporting). You can reset or delete your advertising ID and manage these ad settings in your phone's settings (Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads, depending on the phone).
  • Age: at the start of the ads the game asks your birth year once, neutrally. Only the group is kept on the phone (under 13, 13–17, adult), never the year; a birth year that could fall in two groups counts in the younger one. Under 13, Google's child treatment applies (no personalized ads, no advertising identifier, no third-party ad vendors); 13–17, its teen treatment (no personalized ads). Every ad is rated "G" (general audience) at most.
  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks before ads are requested; Settings → About → Privacy choices changes the answer later.
  • Without an internet connection there are no ads, and the game plays as usual.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you have a Google Play Games profile, the game signs in to it by itself when it starts, and reports your achievement progress, your Thorn trial clear times and your close-call counts to it. Google keeps them and shows them to you and, on the leaderboards, to other players under your Play Games name, as your profile's visibility allows. Google's privacy policy covers them; you can see and delete them in your Play Games profile. The game sends nothing to me and does not read your name, email or friends. Without a Play Games profile nothing is sent and nothing in the game is locked.

Rating the game

After some gardens the game may show Google Play's own rating card. Whether you rate, and what you write, goes to Google Play under your Google account; the game cannot see it.

Reminders (optional)

After the first level won, Android asks once whether the game may send notifications (Android 13 and later; older phones allow them by themselves, and the game asks in one line instead). A yes sets a few silent local notes for the days away (2, 5 and 10 days after the last visit, at most two in any seven days, during the day); they are made on the phone, with no server and no push token, and Settings → Reminders turns them off. To keep them after the phone restarts, the game is allowed to run briefly at startup.

Permissions

Notifications (asked, see above), vibration and running at startup (for the reminders) are the game's own. Google's ad software adds internet and network access and the advertising ID. The game asks for no camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

Test versions

Versions given to testers before release (Google Play's closed testing) also keep a play log on the phone: levels started, cleared and lost, attempts, hits and falls, when the hint hand appeared, whether the opening was watched or skipped, time played, the days the game was opened, the phone model and Android version, and whether an ad was shown or skipped (and why), Google's consent answer and the age group (under 13 or not). It holds no name, email or contacts, is left out of backups and is never sent anywhere: a tester can share it by hand from Settings → Test log. The version on Google Play keeps no play log.

Children

The game is made for everyone and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. For players under 13 the ads follow Google's child treatment described above.

Changes and contact
