Naila’s Orbit

A quiet one-tap orbit game for iPhone: tap once to leap from orbit to orbit, slip past danger by a hair and make the bud bloom.

Coming to the App Store for iPhone
  • 200 handcrafted levels in 10 moss gardens; every garden brings a new rule
  • Near misses shown to the hundredth of a second, in slow motion
  • Gold Bloom, optional Thorn trials and a daily garden
  • Plays in English, Azerbaijani, Turkish, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Russian and Brazilian Portuguese
  • No account, plays offline; short ads after some wins, and one purchase turns them off for good
